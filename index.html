How it works

Start learning in three simple steps

1

Choose a book

Import an ebook in your target language or generate a short story with AI. Start with something you're excited to read.

2

Read & tap

Read at your own pace. When you encounter unfamiliar words, tap them for instant translations. Context makes learning stick.

3

Study & grow

Between reading sessions, review your saved vocabulary with interactive exercises. Reinforce what you've learned naturally.
